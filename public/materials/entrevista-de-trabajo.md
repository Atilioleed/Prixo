# Entrevista de trabajo en inglés (B2)

## Preguntas que casi siempre aparecen

- "Tell me about yourself."
- "Why do you want to work here?"
- "What's your greatest strength / weakness?"
- "Where do you see yourself in five years?"
- "Tell me about a time you faced a conflict at work and how you handled it."
- "Why should we hire you over other candidates?"

## Cómo estructurar una respuesta de ejemplo (método STAR)

- **Situation** — el contexto: "In my previous role at..."
- **Task** — qué se esperaba de vos: "I was responsible for..."
- **Action** — qué hiciste: "So I decided to..."
- **Result** — el resultado, con números si podés: "As a result, we increased... by 20%."

## Preguntas para hacerle al entrevistador

- "What does success look like in this role after the first six months?"
- "What's the team structure I'd be working with?"
- "What are the next steps in the process?"

## Frases para sonar seguro sin sonar memorizado

- "That's a great question — let me think about a specific example."
- "If I'm being honest, one area I'm actively working on is..."
- "I'd love to know more about..."

## Practica

Hacé una entrevista simulada para un puesto real que te interese. Respondé al
menos dos preguntas usando la estructura STAR.
