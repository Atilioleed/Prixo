# Viajar sin traductor — aeropuerto y hotel (B1)

## Check-in en el aeropuerto

- "I'd like to check in for my flight to [destino], please."
- "Do you have an aisle or window seat available?"
- "How many bags am I allowed to check?"
- "My flight got delayed — can you help me rebook my connection?"

## Control de seguridad y migración

- "I have nothing to declare."
- "I'm here for tourism / business, staying for [X] days."
- "Do I need to take off my shoes / laptop out of the bag?"

## Llegando al hotel

- "I have a reservation under the name [nombre]."
- "Is breakfast included in the rate?"
- "What time is check-out?"
- "Could you recommend somewhere to eat nearby?"
- "The air conditioning isn't working — could someone take a look?"

## Vocabulario clave

- **boarding pass** — tarjeta de embarque
- **layover** — escala
- **carry-on** — equipaje de mano
- **front desk** — recepción
- **wake-up call** — llamada para despertar

## Practica

Simula el check-in de un vuelo con una escala, y después el check-in en el hotel
al llegar tarde en la noche. Usá al menos 5 frases de esta lista.
