# Business English — reuniones (B2)

## Frases para abrir una reunión

- "Let's get started, shall we?"
- "Thanks everyone for joining today."
- "The purpose of this meeting is to…"

## Negociar y dar opiniones

- "I see your point, but have you considered…?"
- "That works for us, on one condition…"
- "We'd need to review the numbers before committing."
- "Let's find a middle ground here."

## Cerrar una reunión

- "Let's recap the action items."
- "I'll follow up with an email by Friday."
- "Looking forward to moving this forward."

## Practica

Arma un roleplay de negociación de precio con tu tutor de IA usando al menos 3 frases de arriba.
