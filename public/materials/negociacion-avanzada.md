# Negociación avanzada con proveedores (C1)

## Abrir la negociación sin ceder terreno

- "We're looking to build a long-term relationship, so let's find a number that works for both sides."
- "Before we talk price, can you walk me through your cost breakdown?"
- "That's higher than we budgeted for — help me understand what's driving that."

## Tácticas para pedir mejores condiciones

- "If we commit to a larger volume, is there room to move on price?"
- "What would it take to get better payment terms — net 60 instead of net 30?"
- "We'd need a firmer lead time guarantee to justify that price point."

## Manejar objeciones sin quedar mal parado

- "I understand your position, but here's why that doesn't work on our end..."
- "Let's park that point for now and come back to it once we've covered the rest."
- "I hear you — what if we met in the middle at [número]?"

## Cerrar el trato

- "So, to confirm: [resumen de los términos]. Does that match your understanding?"
- "Let's get this in writing so both sides are aligned."
- "I appreciate you working with us on this — looking forward to a strong partnership."

## Vocabulario clave

- **lead time** — tiempo de entrega
- **payment terms** — condiciones de pago
- **markup** — margen de ganancia
- **to sweeten the deal** — mejorar la oferta
- **to walk away from the table** — retirarse de la negociación

## Practica

Simula negociar el precio de un pedido de 10.000 unidades con un proveedor que
no quiere ceder en el precio inicial. Intentá conseguir mejores condiciones de
pago aunque no logres bajar el precio.
