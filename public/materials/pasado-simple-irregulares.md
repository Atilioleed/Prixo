# Pasado simple — verbos irregulares (A2)

Los verbos irregulares no siguen la regla de agregar "-ed". Hay que memorizarlos.

| Verbo (presente) | Pasado    | Traducción |
|-------------------|-----------|------------|
| go                | went      | ir         |
| eat                | ate       | comer      |
| buy                | bought    | comprar    |
| see                | saw       | ver        |
| have               | had       | tener      |
| do                 | did       | hacer      |
| say                | said      | decir      |
| get                | got       | obtener    |

## Ejemplos

- I **went** to the store yesterday.
- She **bought** some fruits and bread.
- We **saw** a great movie last weekend.

## Practica

Escribe 5 oraciones sobre tu fin de semana usando estos verbos en pasado.
