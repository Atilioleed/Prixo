# Mi familia y mis colores favoritos (A1 — niños)

## Mi familia

- **mom / dad** — mamá / papá
- **brother / sister** — hermano / hermana
- **grandma / grandpa** — abuela / abuelo
- "This is my mom. Her name is [nombre]."
- "I have two sisters and one brother."

## Los colores

- red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink, black, white
- "My favorite color is blue."
- "The sun is yellow."

## Los números del 1 al 10

- one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten
- "I am eight years old."

## Canción corta para practicar

"I have a family, a family, a family.
I have a family, I love them very much!"
(Repetir cambiando "family" por "mom", "dad", "sister", etc.)

## Practica

Dibujá a tu familia y contale al tutor quién es cada uno, usando "This is my...".
Después contale cuál es tu color favorito y por qué.
